About us

Clean water, delivered simply

RIA Pure Water supplies water dispensers, filtration systems, and commercial water equipment to homes, offices, and businesses across Malaysia.

Who we are

Draft — rewrite in your own voice

RIA Pure Water is the public brand of RIA Home Sdn Bhd, a Malaysian business built around one straightforward idea — everyone deserves clean, reliable drinking water without the fuss.

We stock a full range: countertop and floor-standing dispensers for the home, undersink and smart-faucet systems for the kitchen, whole-house filtration for the whole family, and commercial equipment for offices, restaurants, and hospitality.

We handle delivery and installation across Malaysia, and we're always a WhatsApp message away if you need advice picking the right unit.

What we sell

Home Water Dispensers

Countertop, floor-standing, bottle-type, portable

Undersink & Smart Faucets

Undersink systems, smart touch faucets, alkaline

Commercial Equipment

Water boilers, coolers, hospitality appliances

Whole-House Filtration

Master filters (FRP + SS), outdoor UF systems

Filters & Cartridges

Replacement ceramic cartridges, filter housings

Parts & Fittings

Fittings, faucets, pressure tanks, valves, timers
